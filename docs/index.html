Privacy, filed in plain language

Your study material is not our business.

Corvus Recall has no developer account system, analytics SDK, ad network, or tracking. Optional iCloud features use your private Apple container and stay off until you enable them.

  • Core study works without a network connection.
  • Photos and files are read only after you choose them.
  • OCR and handwriting recognition run on the device.
  • Nearby sessions use encrypted peer-to-peer connections.
  • Exports happen only when you request them.
